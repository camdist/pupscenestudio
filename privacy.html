Basic Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the basic privacy practices for the PupScene Studio beta operated by Campo Digital Studio.

1. Information you enter into the app

PupScene Studio lets you enter product information, storylines, prompts, target-audience notes, product links, and other project details. In the current beta, project text is primarily processed in your browser and may be stored in your browser's local storage so your draft can be restored.

2. Reference images

Character, location, and product reference images selected in PupScene are used locally to preview and prepare your project workflow. The beta does not automatically upload those local image files to Campo Digital Studio. When you open an external AI video provider, you decide whether to upload the images there, and that provider's own privacy terms apply.

3. External AI providers

PupScene can help you prepare prompts for third-party services such as Google Flow, Canva, Higgsfield, and Runway. PupScene does not control those services. If you open or use them, their own privacy policies, account requirements, cookies, data practices, subscription terms, and AI-processing rules apply.

4. Feedback

If you voluntarily send feedback by email, we may receive the information you include, such as your email address, device/browser details, and message. We use that information to troubleshoot, improve the beta, and respond when appropriate.

5. Local storage and clearing data

The beta may use browser local storage for your working project, intro preferences, and related app state. You can clear the current project from within the app, and you may also clear site data using your browser settings. Clearing browser data may permanently remove locally stored PupScene project information.

6. Analytics and future changes

The beta may later add basic analytics or account features to understand usage and improve the product. If data practices materially change, this policy should be updated before those features are used broadly.

7. Children

PupScene Studio is intended for creators and business users and is not designed as a service directed to children under 13.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@pupscenestudio.site once the PupScene Studio domain email is active.

Important: this is a basic beta privacy policy and not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before a paid commercial launch, the final policy should be reviewed against the countries where you sell and any analytics, payment, account, or cloud services you add.
