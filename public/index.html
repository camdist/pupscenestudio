      Privacy Policy

      Effective September 24, 2026

      1. What PupScene stores

      PupScene Studio stores your current draft, saved Project Library entries, and prompt edits in your browser's local storage so you can return to your work. These project records stay on the device and browser profile where you created them unless you export and move them yourself.

      2. Reference images

      Character, location, and product reference images selected in PupScene are used locally for previews and filenames. PupScene does not automatically upload those files to Campo Digital Studio. For browser-security reasons, reference files are not persisted in the Project Library and must be selected again after reopening a project.

      3. External AI providers

      When you choose Google Flow, Canva, Higgsfield, Runway, or another external service, PupScene copies or prepares content for that provider and opens its website. Anything you upload or submit there is handled under that provider's own privacy policy and terms. PupScene does not control their data practices.

      4. Feedback and contact

      The current Feedback feature prepares a message in your device's email application. PupScene does not receive your feedback until you choose to send that email. If you provide an email address in the feedback text, it is included only in the message you send.

      5. Cookies and analytics

      PupScene uses secure account sessions and stores account, plan, storyboard-usage, and billing-status records needed to provide paid access. Authentication cookies are HttpOnly and used only to keep you signed in. Advertising cookies are not required by the core app.

      6. Your choices

      You can delete individual saved projects from the Project Library, clear the current draft with Reset, or clear site storage through your browser settings. Clearing browser/site data can permanently remove locally saved projects, so export important projects first.

      7. Contact

      PupScene Studio is operated by Campo Digital Studio. Planned website: pupscenestudio.site. Planned support contact: support@pupscenestudio.site.
